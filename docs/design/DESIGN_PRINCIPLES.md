# Design Principles

Rolevia UI is technical, calm, fast, dense, and human. We take conceptual inspiration from the design quality of Linear, Raycast, and Stripe without copying their designs.

---

## 1. Aesthetic Tenets
* **Calm & Focused**: Avoid garish neon gradients and visual clutter.
* **Information Density**: Optimized for professional SaaS workflows, dense data grids, and structured side-by-side editing.
* **Purposeful Motion**: Micro-animations indicate state changes (< 150ms). Never slow down the candidate's task.
* **Truthful UI States**: Distinct, prominent visual states for loading, empty, unverified, and verified data.
