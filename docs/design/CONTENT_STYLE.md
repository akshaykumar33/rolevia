# Content & Voice Guidelines

Communication within Rolevia is concise, technical, objective, and candid.

---

## Voice Principles
* **Direct & Objective**: "Missing requirement: 3+ years Go experience" instead of "Oops! You don't have enough Go experience yet!"
* **Zero Marketing Fluff in Dashboards**: Do not use celebratory popups for routine actions.
* **Respectful Transparency**: Clearly state AI confidence levels without condescension or exaggeration.
