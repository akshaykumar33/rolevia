# User Personas

Rolevia supports distinct human personas operating across personal and organizational scopes.

---

## Persona Directory

### 1. Anonymous Visitor
* **Goal**: Understand product value, explore public career insights, review security & privacy postures, sign up.
* **Access**: Public pages, documentation overview, marketing landing.

### 2. Individual Candidate
* **Goal**: Optimize personal career progression, upload resumes/achievements, discover high-signal job matches, tailor applications, track interviews, and prepare.
* **Access**: Personal workspace, candidate profile, Career Intelligence Vault, job engine, applications studio.

### 3. Organization Candidate
* **Goal**: A candidate sponsored by an educational institution, bootcamp, or outplacement program. Receives organization mentorship while retaining personal career privacy.
* **Access**: Workspace within the organization tenant; can optionally share tailored application materials with designated mentors.

### 4. Mentor / Career Advisor
* **Goal**: Guide candidates, review resume revisions, provide interview feedback, suggest targeted roles.
* **Access**: Read-only or advisory view over assigned candidate workspaces. Cannot submit applications on behalf of candidates without explicit consent.

### 5. Organization Admin
* **Goal**: Manage institutional cohorts, monitor aggregate placement rates, provision mentor licenses, configure organizational SSO.
* **Access**: Organization tenant administration, aggregate reporting dashboards.

### 6. Platform Administrator
* **Goal**: Monitor platform operations, investigate system incidents, review connector health, inspect anonymized telemetry.
* **Access**: Internal administration portal with strict multi-factor authentication and audit logging.

### 7. Platform Owner
* **Goal**: System architecture evolution, licensing, global infrastructure management, security policy enforcement.
* **Access**: Full administrative governance.
