# Non-Goals

Documenting what Rolevia explicitly will NOT do is critical to preserving architectural focus, user trust, and security posture.

---

## Explicit Non-Goals

1. **Mass Unsolicited "Shotgun" Job Applications**:
   Rolevia will NOT provide a "spam 1,000 jobs in one click" feature. Spray-and-pray applications damage candidate reputation, overwhelm recruiters, and lead to poor placement outcomes.
2. **Credential Theft & Security Circumvention**:
   Rolevia will NEVER implement CAPTCHA-bypassing solvers, session hijacking, or anti-bot circumvention exploits. Integrations must use legitimate APIs, official partner connectors, or user-assisted workflows.
3. **AI Credential Fabrication**:
   The AI will NEVER fabricate years of experience, fake job titles, unearned degrees, or untrue accomplishments. Any attempt by automated prompts to hallucinate unverified claims is blocked by the validation layer.
4. **Autonomous Account Takeover**:
   The system will NEVER alter user security credentials, delete historical career data, or bind paid subscriptions without active, authenticated user confirmation.
5. **Universal Headhunter / Employer CRM**:
   Rolevia is candidate-first. It is not an ATS for employers or a mass recruitment database for headhunters.
