# Product Vision

## Mission Statement
Rolevia is a secure, multi-tenant AI Career Operating System that understands a candidate, maintains persistent career intelligence, discovers relevant jobs, evaluates fit, generates truthful tailored application material, assists or automates permitted application workflows, tracks outcomes, prepares candidates for interviews, and continuously improves the user's career strategy.

---

## Core Philosophy & Principles

1. **Truthful Career Representation**: AI is a partner in articulation and translation, not a fabricator of credentials. Fabricating qualifications, certifications, or metrics is an absolute violation of system integrity.
2. **Persistent Career Memory**: A job search should not start from scratch every time. Career intelligence accumulates, learns from interview feedback, and tracks long-term trajectory.
3. **Transparent Matching**: Match scores are never a black box. The system clearly details exact requirements met, partial matches, missing competencies, and strategic positioning opportunities.
4. **Candidate Sovereignty**: The user owns their career data, controls when and where applications are submitted, and grants explicit consent for any automated activity.
