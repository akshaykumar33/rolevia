# Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope

The initial release targets individual candidates seeking high-precision career assistance without sacrificing data sovereignty.

---

## In Scope (MVP)

1. **User Authentication & Personal Workspace**: Email/password + OAuth (GitHub/Google) with tenant isolation.
2. **Career Intelligence Vault**: PDF resume ingestion, extraction into structured skills/experiences, and user confirmation UI.
3. **Target Job Analysis**: Manual paste or URL ingestion of a Job Description with requirement parsing.
4. **Fit Breakdown & Matching**: Transparent comparison of candidate verified evidence against JD requirements.
5. **Truthful Resume Tailoring**: Generation of tailored resume sections strictly grounded in confirmed candidate facts with provenance citations.
6. **Application Tracker**: Kanban/list tracking of applications, interview dates, and outcomes.
7. **Basic Export**: Exporting tailored resume as cleanly formatted PDF and Markdown.

---

## Deferred Beyond MVP

- Automated third-party browser submissions (Phase 8)
- Multi-seat organization / university placement admin portals (Phase 12)
- Live audio mock interviews (Phase 10)
- Multi-source real-time web scraping connectors (Phase 7)
